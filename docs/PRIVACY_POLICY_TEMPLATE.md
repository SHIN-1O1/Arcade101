## Summary
Arcade 101 ("the app") is an offline collection of puzzle and arcade games. Your game progress,
stars and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers, accounts or analytics,
and we do not collect your name, email or location.

## Ads
The free version shows ads served by **Google AdMob** (Google LLC). To show and measure ads,
Google and its partners may collect and use:
- your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- device information (model, OS version, language),
- how you interact with ads.

Depending on where you live, we ask for your consent before ads may use this data, using Google's
consent form (User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice any time in **Settings > Privacy
settings** (shown where required) and reset your advertising ID in Android settings
(*Settings > Google > Ads*). Google's use of data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

The app is **not directed to children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Purchases
The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by **Google Play**. We receive confirmation that you
own the item; we never see your payment details. Use Settings > Restore purchases after reinstalling.

## Data stored on your device
Game progress, settings and the "ads removed" flag are saved in the app's private storage and
deleted when you uninstall the app. Nothing is sent to us.

## Your rights

processing of personal data. Because we hold no personal data, contact Google for data it holds
through ads: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543 . For anything else, email [CONTACT EMAIL].
